# Third-party notices

## Runtime and build dependencies

Race Replay Lab uses third-party libraries declared in `pom.xml`, including
OpenJFX, Jackson and JUnit. Maven resolves them from their respective published
artifacts. Their own license terms apply.

Before each public release, generate and review a Software Bill of Materials
and a dependency-license report. Release packages must include all notices
required by the exact dependency versions they contain.
